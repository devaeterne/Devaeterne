## 👨‍💻 About Me

I'm a **Full-Stack Engineer and Software Architect** focused on building scalable business applications, operational platforms, and data-driven systems.

I enjoy working on projects where software goes beyond the interface — systems involving complex business rules, database design, permissions, workflows, integrations, automation, and real-world operations.

My approach is simple:

> **Understand the business first. Design the system second. Write the code third.**

I care about clean architecture, strong data models, maintainable code, predictable deployments, and building software that can evolve without becoming fragile.

---

## 🚀 What I'm Building

Currently, I'm working on a modern **warehouse, inventory, and product management ecosystem** designed around real operational workflows.

The platform includes concepts such as:

* QR-based warehouse operations
* Product and SKU management
* Warehouse, zone, shelf, and location tracking
* Inventory movement and stock control
* Flexible customer pricing architectures
* Role-based administration
* Customer-facing product catalogs
* Cloud-native authentication and storage
* Operational dashboards and internal tools
* Scalable PostgreSQL data modeling

The stack combines **Next.js, React, TypeScript, PostgreSQL, Supabase, and Vercel** with an architecture designed to grow into a larger modular business platform.

---

## 🧠 How I Think About Software

I don't see software development as simply connecting a frontend to a database.

I'm especially interested in:

**System Architecture**
Designing boundaries, modules, data relationships, permissions, workflows, and systems that remain understandable as they grow.

**Business Logic**
Turning complicated operational requirements into clear and maintainable software models.

**Database Design**
Building relational schemas that represent the business correctly instead of forcing business logic into application code.

**Developer Experience**
Keeping projects structured, predictable, deployable, and easy to continue developing months later.

**Automation**
Removing unnecessary manual work through smart workflows, integrations, and internal tooling.

**AI Engineering**
Exploring practical applications of LLMs, NLP pipelines, intelligent automation, and AI-assisted software systems.
